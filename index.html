Hello there, I'm Moor!

I just became a railway worker at *Desert*, a tiny railway station... well, in the middle of a vast desert.

It is sooo vast that the sand seems endless on either side of the railway.

Nobody knows what lies beyond the sand.

My coworkers have warned me that this is going to be a very boring and lonely place to work at.

I guess my only chance for fun is to save enough Dunaris to travel.

Waking up to the blazing desert sun today, I just felt totally wiped out and lonely.

Seeing nothing but sand everywhere just reminds me how alone I am out here, draining all my energy.

Every time I make sure a train comes and goes on time, it just makes me feel even more isolated at this lonely desert stop.

Checking the tracks for stuff blocking them feels like a never-ending job, kinda like the emptiness I feel in this huge, empty place.

The sun's just relentless, sucking out whatever energy I got left and making me feel even more isolated and tired.

Dust keeps swirling around me in this lonely dance with the desert wind, matching how exhausted I feel inside.

Thought being alone in the peaceful desert would help, but it's just leaving me feeling even more tired.

Trains bring some life here, but when they leave, it's like they take all the energy and leave me feeling even more drained and alone.

Nights in the desert should be peaceful, but instead, I just feel super lonely and exhausted, wishing for some relief.

Just me and endless sand, facing another day feeling so alone and tired.

What a day of work!

What a day of travel!
